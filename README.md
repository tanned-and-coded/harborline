Harborline is an original 2D top-down open-world crime game set in Port Azura, a sun-soaked coastal city of palm-lined streets, busy markets and a working harbour with a pier over open water. You play Jett Marlowe, a courier who takes on a four-mission story chain — a timed parcel delivery, a borrowed-car delivery, a dockside drop under rising police heat, and a final escape to the pier — each with on-screen objectives, countdown timers and cash payouts. Roam six named districts (Old Market, Palm Heights, North Yards, Marina Row, City Centre, Dockside and The Harbour) on foot or behind the wheel: steal any parked or moving car, weave through waypoint-driven traffic, and watch pedestrians stroll, flee danger and drop cash. Crimes raise a five-star wanted level; police cars spawn off-screen, pursue, intercept and ram, and heat cools only while you stay out of sight. Fight with melee swings and, once earned in the story, a mouse-aimed pistol. The HUD shows your district, objective and timer, health and cash, wanted stars, and a live minimap with roads, water, police blips, cash drops and the mission marker. A title screen with New Game / Continue, pause, an original "DOWN AND OUT" game-over screen with revive, synthesised WebAudio sound effects and localStorage saving round out the game — all static files, no build step, fully offline.

Controls: WASD / arrow keys move and drive, Shift runs, E enters or exits (steals) a car, F punches, mouse aims and click shoots once armed, P or Esc pauses, N / C / R / Enter confirm menu choices.

## Run it

node serve.ts, then open http://localhost:8080
